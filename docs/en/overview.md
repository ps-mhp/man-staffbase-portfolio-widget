# Portfolio

The Portfolio widget displays a series of tiles side by side. Each tile
contains an image and a title, and when clicked, leads to a page—either within
the app or externally. It’s ideal for any situation where multiple objectives of equal importance
destinations should be displayed side by side: product areas, departments, contact points.

Staffbase includes such a widget, but only in the Classic Editor. This
widget is a replica for the Studio Editor. It looks exactly the same and can
also display a category label above the title.

## What Readers See

A grid of tiles of equal width that adjusts to the width of the screen
. Two display options are available:

- **Image Tile** — an image at the top, followed by, depending on the setup, a small
  category label, the title, and a short description.
- **Icon Box** — an icon and the title centered on a colored background,
  set by default to MAN red with white text.

Both are available in three sizes: **Compact** (many small tiles per row),
**Normal**, and **Large** (image on the left, text on the right, like a news list).
The background and text can be customized to match the MAN CI colors or chosen freely.
An arrow appears in the upper right corner as long as the tile has a link.

When you hover over it with the mouse, the border becomes bolder and the title and arrow
turn red; on a colored background, the background instead becomes slightly darker. The entire tile is the link, not just the title.

## What You See in the CMS Editor

In the editor, the tiles appear exactly as they do on the page. However, the links
are not functional there—open the preview to check the destinations.

The tiles are managed in the full-screen **Edit Tiles** window: on the left
is the list of tiles, in the middle are the fields for the selected tile, and on the right is the
design (layout, size, colors, arrow). You can drag the tiles to reorder them in the list, and a
preview shows the row at desktop, tablet, and mobile widths. Everything is saved
in a single attribute; widgets from earlier versions continue to be recognized.
